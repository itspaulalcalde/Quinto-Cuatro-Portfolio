Collage

THIS PIECE REFLECTS ON THE IDEA THAT RELIGIOUS BELIEF IS ROOTED IN THE STRUCTURE AND FUNCTION OF THE HUMAN BRAIN. RATHER THAN UNDERSTANDING FAITH AS SOMETHING EXTERNAL OR DIVINE, IT PROPOSES THAT SPIRITUAL EXPERIENCES EMERGE FROM COGNITIVE PROCESSES SHAPED BY EVOLUTION. IT QUESTIONS THE NATURE OF BELIEF, SUGGESTING THAT WHAT WE PERCEIVE AS TRANSCENDENCE MAY BE THE RESULT OF NEURAL ACTIVITY AND THE BRAIN´S NEED TO CREATE MEANING.

THIS PIECE EXPLORES THE MORAL AND STRUCTURAL IMBALANCE OF MODERN FINANCIAL SYSTEMS. IT REFELCTS A WORLD DRIVEN BY PROFIT, WHERE RISK, EXCESS AND SPECULATION REPLACE STABILITY AND ETHICAL RESPONSABILITY. INSPIRED BY THE DYNAMICS OF WALL STREET, THE WORK CRITIQUES A SYSTEM IN WHICH ECONOMIC POWER OPERATES WITH LITTLE ACCOUNTABILITY, WHILE ITS CONSEQUENCES EXTEND FAR BEYOND THOSE WHO CONTROL IT.

Branding

TOTE BAG DESIGN INSPIRED BY CONTEMPORARY AND POP ART AESTHETICS. THE COMPOSITION FEATURES REPEATED ICONIC IMAGERY COMBINED WITH BOLD TYPOGRAPHY TO CREATE A STRONG AND RECOGNIZABLE VISUAL IDENTITY. THE DESIGN EXTENDS THE MUSEUM EXPERIENCE INTO AN EVERYDAY OBJECT, BLENDING FUNCTIONALITY WITH ARTISTIC EXPRESSION.

THIS PROMOTIONAL POSTER WAS DESIGNED TO ADVERTISE MOCO MUSEUM AND ITS COLLECTION OF MODERN AND CONTEMPORARY ART. THROUGH THE USE OF BOLD TYPOGRAPHY, SOFT GRADIENTS AND ORGANIC FORMS, THE DESIGN CREATES A STRIKING VISUAL IDENTITY THAT CAPTURES THE MUSEUM'S CREATIVE AND INNOVATIVE SPIRIT. THE COMPOSITION SEEKS TO ENGAGE THE VIEWER WHILE COMMUNICATING A SENSE OF CULTURAL RELEVANCE AND ARTISTIC EXPRESSION.

Short film

Permanente

ON THE EVE OF A LIFE-CHANGING AUDITION, AGNES, A YOUNG ACTRESS, SPENDS THE NIGHT REHEARSING IN FRONT OF A MIRROR. AS SHE REPEATS HER LINES AND CAREFULLY SHAPES EVERY MOVEMENT, THE BOUNDARY BETWEEN CHARACTER AND SELF BEGINS TO FADE. WHAT STARTS AS A RIGOROUS PREPARATION SOON UNFOLDS INTO A COMEDIC AND UNPREDICTABLE JOURNEY, EXPOSING THE INSECURITIES, EXPECTATIONS AND CONTRADICTIONS HIDDEN BEHIND THE ACT OF PERFORMING. BOTH PLAYFUL AND INTIMATE, THE FILM REFLECTS ON THE SEARCH FOR PERFECTION AND THE FRAGILE RELATIONSHIP WE MAINTAIN WITH OUR OWN SELF-IMAGE.

Cine-Filia

A SHORT FILM THAT EXPLORES THE IDEA OF LOVE AT FIRST SIGHT THROUGH A YOUNG WOMAN'S ENCOUNTER WITH CINEMA. AFTER WATCHING MARTIN SCORSESE'S TAXI DRIVER FOR THE FIRST TIME, SHE EXPERIENCES AN IMMEDIATE AND PROFOUND CONNECTION THAT GOES FAR BEYOND SIMPLE ADMIRATION. WHAT BEGINS AS A FILM SCREENING GRADUALLY EVOLVES INTO A TRANSFORMATIVE EXPERIENCE, AWAKENING A NEW WAY OF SEEING, FEELING AND UNDERSTANDING THE WORLD AROUND HER.
